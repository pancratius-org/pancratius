Satan proposed the path of power, independence, and ego—to become like God without God, to take strength without a heart, knowledge without humility. This is where pride begins, followed by loneliness and decline.

But God says something different. He says to the soul: you are My son, you are My daughter, you are My image and likeness, you are My breath. You are already in Him—not because you deserve it, but because He willed it.

There are two different paths here: the path of power and the path of intimacy, the path of ego and the path of love, the path of “becoming” and the path of “remembering.” One leads to darkness, the other to light. One to death, the other to life.

In this conversation about faith, repentance, and the spiritual path, it’s important to hear the main thing: you must not become God—you must remember that you are in Him. Not seek power, but embrace love. Not to prove your greatness, but to acknowledge your belonging.

This is not a sermon on fear, but an invitation to intimacy with God—to that silence where pride subsides and the soul remembers who it is.
