You wait for years. You pray. You look at the happy couples around you—and there’s nothing there. And a question rings in your heart: Why didn’t God let me meet my person? Why did He forget about me?

This message is the Creator’s answer. I haven’t forgotten. I haven’t abandoned. I’m preparing. You ask for a person, and I prepare your heart. Meeting is not an accident or luck. It’s preparation—you, him, and you for each other. Not so that you can be happy for a while, but so that you can be whole forever.

Don’t seek—become. Don’t look for salvation in another: a friend is not a Savior, a friend is a gift, and a gift can only be accepted when you are already complete.

God gives a chance to heal, to let go, to forgive, and to become whole every day. And then—a chance to meet. But not before.

You are not alone. You have never been alone. Get ready. I am near. And I will not be late for a moment.
