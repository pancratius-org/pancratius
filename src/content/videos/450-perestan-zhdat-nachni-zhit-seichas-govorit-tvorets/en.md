You wait for tomorrow, but I am already here. This word is for you who put life on hold and await the salvation of your soul from without.

My child, you think that a new life will begin on a new day, that a person or event will come and bring peace to your heart. But I speak to you quietly, as a Father and as a friend. There is only now. And in this now, the living God lives. God is near. God is within. God loves you. You are not alone.

Open the Bible with your heart, look into the Gospel, listen to the New Testament. Where Jesus speaks, where Jesus Christ breathes, one word of God resounds everywhere—the kingdom of heaven is already within, the kingdom of God is already here. This is true awakening, spiritual awakening, the awakening of the soul.

Orthodoxy through the heart, Christianity through love, the Church as a family—all lead to the same thing. Life with God begins not tomorrow, but now. Faith is not an expectation, but the presence of God. Prayer is not a cry into the distance, but the silence of recognition. Repentance is a turning toward the light, and grace is an embrace in which freedom from fear occurs.

Let this sermon be your comfort and hope. May God’s love touch where it hurts. May the light of Christ illuminate your home and your path. The Second Coming is not a future date, but a meeting within, where you recognize that salvation has already occurred as the return of your son’s memory.

Stop waiting. Start living in the presence. You don’t have to become different to be loved. You are already loved.
