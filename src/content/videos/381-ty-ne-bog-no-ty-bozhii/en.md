Humility is not humiliation. It is honesty before God.
“Lord, I am not God. I am not the source of life. I am Yours.”

This is God’s message: stop being God to yourself.
God’s words today: all good in me is from Him. All sickness leads to healing.
This is faith and Christianity without pride and without self-destruction.

Prayer and the Gospel resound here—not ritual, but breathing.
This is the awareness of the truth: God is God. I am His creation. Christ is the Savior.
The Spirit is my life. The Father is my home.

This self-development is not about “becoming more,” but about “getting down to earth.”
You are not God—and that is not defeat, but freedom.
You are God’s—and that is not pride, but peace.

And everything in me that wants to be more than this must be silenced.
And everything that fears being loved must hear: do not be afraid.
