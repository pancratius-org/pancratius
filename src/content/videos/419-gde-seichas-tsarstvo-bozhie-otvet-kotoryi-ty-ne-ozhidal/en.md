You ask, “When will Your Kingdom come?”
You look at wars. At suffering. At divisions. And you think the Kingdom must one day enter this world, like the sun rising after a long night.

This video contains an answer that changes everything you knew about the Second Coming:
The Kingdom of God does not come in a visible way. It does not begin with great events. It begins with a heart that has ceased to live apart from love.

What this video is about: - Why the Kingdom of God is already within you—and will not come “later” - Where the old man ends—and a new one is born - How one man, having decided to love without reward, launches the Kingdom - Why love does not howl, but unfolds—and this changes everything

Don’t ask “when.” Ask, “Lord, can Your Kingdom become visible through me today?”

Because that’s how it comes into the world. Not from the top down. But from the inside out.
