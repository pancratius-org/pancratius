Man waits for thunder, but fails to hear the quiet call of conscience.
Waits for a great sign, but fails to see the child’s plea.
Waits for revelation, but fails to read the open Gospel.

God often comes hidden.
Not because He’s playing hide-and-seek.
But because love respects the freedom and depth of man.

Too much external brightness can overwhelm.
Quietness invites a response from the heart.

This is God’s message for those who are tired of waiting for signs.
This is Christianity without special effects: seeing God in bread, in the face of a neighbor, in a repentant thought.
Mindfulness: the quiet presence of God—in the morning light, in fatigue, in pain, in joy.

The ultimate simplicity of faith: God is closer than you think.
The Lord doesn’t always come as you expect.
Jesus taught: the Kingdom of God is within you.

This is the healing of the soul from the endless wait for “real” revelation.
The Bible is open. The bread is on the table. The cup is nearby.
He is already here.
