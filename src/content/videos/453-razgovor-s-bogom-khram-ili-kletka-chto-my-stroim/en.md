You are stuck in a form, and I am calling you to move on. This is God’s word for those who are tired of external faith without the living presence of God within.

My child, to go is not to do. To go is to respond. Do not stop where you met Me. Many build a temple around the moment and remain in shape, defending rules and judging others, while the light fades.

A priest can become stuck in demands and authority. A monk can withdraw into silence. A layperson can pray out of habit. And even a procession with the cross can become a demonstration of fear if it lacks love. I ask your heart—is it fear or love?

Living faith is a movement born from stillness. Be a light in your surroundings. Serve quietly, without seeking recognition. Wash your feet every day—in word, deed, forgiveness. Don’t isolate yourself. Respond, not act.

Orthodoxy with the heart, Christianity with love, the Church as a family, not a wall. Open the Bible and the Gospel not to argue, but to live. Let prayer become the silence of recognition. Let God’s love be your step. The living God says: Jesus Christ is near. The Kingdom of God is within you. Let your path, your home, your work be with Christ.
