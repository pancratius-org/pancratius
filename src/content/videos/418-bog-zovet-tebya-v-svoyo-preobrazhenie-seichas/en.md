You’re used to thinking of the Transfiguration as just a page of history, an ancient testimony given to the apostles, not to you. However, today I tell you differently: this is not a memory, but a prophecy that is being fulfilled here and now. This is an invitation addressed personally to you.

Look: a transformation did not occur on Mount Tabor. Christ did not become the Light—He revealed the Light that was always within Him. The disciples saw not a miracle, but the true reality hidden behind the surface of flesh. The reality of Divine light that awaits its hour in each of us.

And here’s what this means for your life today:
You, too, are called to spiritual transformation. Not to become someone else, but to reveal to the world who you were truly created to be. Your task is not to kindle the light within yourself, but to stop obscuring the inner light already given to you by the Creator.

This video is about how the Transfiguration of the Lord is not a distant past, but your present path. This is a call:

Stop trying to be holy and start being transparent to grace.

Each morning, remove the veils from your soul so that love can flow through you without distortion.

Each day becomes real, not perfect.

Transformation of life is the moment when you stop hiding the light within. This is the path to the Kingdom of Heaven, which begins not after death, but right now, on your own Mount Tabor.
