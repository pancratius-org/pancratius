You ask who created God, and I tell you—the Beginning has no beginning. I am, I love you, and I created you to be with you.

My child, you torment your mind with the question of whether God exists and who God is, but I reveal to you the simple: God exists. You cannot find the beginning in the One Who is Himself the Beginning. I am not a link in a chain. I am the Source of the chain. I am not the beginning in time. I am beyond time, in eternity, where there is no before or after.

Your philosophy seeks the cause of causes, your philosophy of God argues about the first cause, and the universe and the Big Bang only hint at the infinity from which everything emanates. Do not seek proof of God in formulas. Accept the mystery as a gift. This is faith—not an explanation, but trust. This is living faith, where hope becomes peace and peace in the heart becomes light.

You ask about life after death and the meaning of life, and I tell you—eternal life is already revealed in My presence near. Hear God’s voice quietly within. My word of God is love. For God’s love does not demand understanding; love calls to remain. Prayer will become breath, Orthodox prayer will become a quiet conversation at home, church will become family, Orthodoxy the heart, Christianity the path home.

Open the Bible with your heart. Hear the Gospel not as a debate, but as the news that the salvation of your soul has already been given to you. Jesus speaks there. Jesus Christ breathes there. God, Jesus Christ, lives there as a brother and Father, who will never abandon you.

You must not know where I came from. You must know that I love you. Remain in trust. I am here.
