You think repentance means getting on your knees and listing your sins. Crying. Beating your chest. Feeling worthless.
You think repentance means humiliation.

This video contains a truth that is rarely spoken. Because repentance is not an act of self-abasement. It is an act of return.

Imagine a son who left home. He lived far away. He made mistakes. He fell. He exhausted himself. And one day he realized, “I want to go home.” He didn’t come with a long list of his mistakes. He came with one: “Father, I want to go back.” And the father didn’t ask him, “What have you done? How many mistakes have you made?” He ran to him. He hugged him. He brought him home.

You can repent with one word. Not long. Not complicated. Not painful. Simple. Honest. Living.
You don’t have to list your mistakes. You must turn to the One who has been waiting for you all this time. That is repentance.
