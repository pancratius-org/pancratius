If you’re familiar with loneliness, this word is for you. There’s a weariness with life, when you’re tired of being on your own, tired of carrying yourself and being strong for everyone. Inside, there’s heartache, emptiness, and the feeling that no one holds you.

This message speaks about what to do with loneliness. Don’t run from it or drown it out with noise, but rather be in silence and hear that loneliness is a calling. A call to encounter God, to a living conversation with God, where prayer becomes not just beautiful words, but an honest coming to you, as it is.

You’ll learn why a person cannot become a source of salvation and why the fear of loneliness disappears when you understand that God is near and God is within you. That His love sustains you in your breath and heartbeat, that His voice resounds in the silence, and that healing the soul begins with encounter, not with struggle.

This spiritual awakening is for those who seek inner peace and want to find peace in their soul, find themselves, and relive the meaning of life. If you say you don’t want to be alone, but don’t know how to cope with loneliness, listen to this word of God from the Conversations with God series.

May the powerful prayer in this message bring you an encounter with God, peace, God’s love, and the assurance that God loves you and you are never alone. Pancratius and the Gospel of the Kingdom are about the living Presence that is already near.
