God is telling you today: stop comparing. You look at others’ gifts, but you don’t see their burden. Every gift is a responsibility. Your measure is not quantitative, but qualitative. You are not deprived. You have been given exactly as much as you need.

You will see: your “less” is your “all.” And that is enough.
