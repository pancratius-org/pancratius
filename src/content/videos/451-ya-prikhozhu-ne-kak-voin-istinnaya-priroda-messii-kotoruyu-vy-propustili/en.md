My child, many imagine the Second Coming as an external force and ask about the timing of Christ’s return. I speak to you quietly, like the voice of God within. The Messiah will not come with a sword to destroy his enemies. Jesus Christ has already shown the way—Jesus washed his feet, forgave those who crucified him, and called us to love our enemies. God is near. God is love, and God loves you.

You study the Bible and the New Testament, you live in a tradition where Orthodoxy and Christianity are important, where the Church and the Gospel are precious. But if Faith has become a rule without love, if Prayer has become a duty without silence, the heart becomes empty. The Law is a bridge, not a goal. Christian life is a quality of love, not a count of commandments. The light of Christ is seen in the fruits—in peace of heart, in forgiveness, in service.

This prophecy is not about fear in the face of images that evoke the Apocalypse and the Antichrist, where judgment is feared, where God’s Judgment is portrayed as wrath, where the Great Tribulation seems like the end. The Second Coming of Jesus Christ is a quiet recognition. The end of times is the end of the age of fear. The Kingdom of Heaven is within. The Living God speaks now through His Word.

Accept this Good News as consolation. God’s love sustains you. The salvation of your soul is already taking place, as is the return of your son’s memory. Let there be a Spiritual Awakening—from weariness to peace, from guilt to freedom, from external expectation to internal encounter.
