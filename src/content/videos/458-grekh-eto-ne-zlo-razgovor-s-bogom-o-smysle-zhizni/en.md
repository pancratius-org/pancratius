What really lies behind the word sin? Discover how the meaning of life changes when you see it not as evil, but as a missed target.

We’re accustomed to perceiving mistakes as something dark, but in the Gospel context, sin is revealed as a simple deviation from the goal. This video from the Conversations with God series offers a different perspective on familiar concepts. Instead of condemnation, you’ll encounter the image of Jesus extending a helping hand, reminding you that every person is a beloved child.

A deep faith in God helps you realize that the path to light is not a struggle against darkness, but a recognition of your own worth. Jesus’ teaching calls us not to fear, but to a conscious return to our essence, even if this path requires courage. Let this spiritual awakening be the beginning of your journey toward accepting your nature.
