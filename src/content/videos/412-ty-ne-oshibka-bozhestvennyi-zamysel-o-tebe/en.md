You want to be valued. You wait for someone to notice you, to tell you that you’re good, that you’ve done well, that you matter. And when that doesn’t happen, you fall. You think, “Then I’m not good enough.”

But here’s what you forget: your value doesn’t need proof.

This video contains a direct word from Jesus Christ that changes everything:
He tells you what you’ve never heard from anyone. Or heard but didn’t believe. You are valuable not because of anything you’ve done. You are valuable because you are. From the beginning of time. To your actions. To your successes and mistakes.

Why you are valuable:
- You are not a biological accident. You are God’s plan all along.
- Your soul is not the result of evolution. It is a gift from the Creator.
- You are not a mistake. You are the embodiment of His idea—He gave you a name before you saw the light of day—You weren’t just planned. You were desired. Loved. Foreseen.

Every time you doubt yourself, you’re not doubting yourself. You’re doubting the Creator. And He doesn’t create marriage. And if you’re here, it means you’re needed. You’re important. You’re valuable.

Not because you tried. But because you exist.

This isn’t a phrase for comfort. It’s the truth. A truth that has always been there—you’ve just rarely been reminded of it.
