Spiritual awakening begins when you stop looking for paradise outside yourself. Eden is not a garden. It is presence. A state in which you know you are loved. Without fear. Without guilt. Without shame.

You think Eden has been lost. But the Holy Spirit speaks to you today: you can already be there. Right now. In your heart.

How to return? Not through works. Not through rituals. Through trust. This is God’s Word for you, Good News you may be hearing for the first time. Returning to Eden means remembering who you are before God.
