You think that value must be earned. That you need to become needed. But the meaning of life isn’t about becoming useful. It’s about knowing: you are already loved.

This video contains the answer that changes everything:
You are not an accident. You are not a mistake. You are not superfluous. You are a design. And I need you. Not for usefulness. For love.

God’s love is like the love of a parent for a child. A parent doesn’t ask: “Why do I need this child? What use is he?” He simply loves because you exist. So it is with me: I created you to love. God is love, and your existence is already the reason for My love.

If you are searching for why I live and why all this—listen: don’t try to be needed. You are needed now. Right now. Just as you are. You are already Mine. This is faith in God and spiritual awakening – not “I must,” but “I am, and I am loved.”
