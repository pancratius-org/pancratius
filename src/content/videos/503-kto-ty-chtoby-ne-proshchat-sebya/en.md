You carry the past like a stone around your neck. Again and again you return to that day and punish yourself, thinking that God sees only your sin and has turned away.

But God says, “I will not punish you. Stop punishing yourself.”

God has already paid on the cross for your sin, for your past, for your tears and your shame. He has already forgiven. He does not remember, does not hold, and does not punish. His love is greater than your sin, His grace is greater than your shame, and His forgiveness is greater than your past.

You don’t have to forgive yourself on your own. Accept forgiveness, trust Him, and let yourself go—not because you deserve it, but because He has given it. Not because you have improved, but because He has already forgiven.

If God has forgiven, who are you not to forgive? If He has set you free, why do you keep yourself imprisoned?
