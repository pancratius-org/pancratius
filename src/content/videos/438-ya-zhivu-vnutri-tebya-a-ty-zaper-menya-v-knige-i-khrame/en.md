I live within you, and you have built temples to keep from entering yourselves. I became breath, and you have made Me into dogma...

You seek the meaning of life far away—in temples, books, rules, signs. But Jesus Christ tells you directly: I am not an idea. Not a law. I AM—within you. You have exchanged love for fulfillment. You have exchanged presence for knowledge. You have exchanged Me for your ideas about Me.

This video contains a rebuke that heals:
— I spoke to you on the street, and you locked Me in a book. — I prayed to the Father for unity, and you divided Me into thousands of denominations. — I said “forgive,” and you said “condemn.” I said “love,” and you said “submit.” — I did not leave, and you continue to wait for My return, not noticing that I am already here. This is the Second Coming—not an event in heaven, but a recognition: He is already here, within. I became Bread, and you turned Me into doctrine. I came to liberate, and you built cages.

Stop. Return. I am not waiting for your prayers—I am waiting for your attention. I am not waiting for your sacrifices—I am waiting for your heart. I am not waiting for your service—I am waiting for you yourself.

This is faith in God and spiritual awakening—to enter within, where God is love. Where the soul finds peace of mind and healing of the soul. Where prayer becomes not speaking for God, but listening to Him. Where the Bible and the Gospel reveal themselves as a living voice, and Christianity and Orthodoxy return to the source—to the living Christ. This is your path to God, your divine love, and your hope
