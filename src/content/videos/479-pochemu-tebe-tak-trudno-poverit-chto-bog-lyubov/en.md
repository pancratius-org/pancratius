This is a message from God about the name of God you almost never pronounce. You know many of God’s names, but you find it difficult to believe that God is love and that God is near you, and that God’s love already holds your soul.

These words convey the voice of God, who does not accuse. He opens the wound to provide healing for the soul.

You will understand why faith in God is extinguished by fear, how to hear God through the noise of condemnation, and how liberation from fear and the path that shows how to be free from fear begin with accepting that God loves you and that God’s love is greater than mistakes.

This is a revelation about God’s being with us, about love that heals the heart, about repentance without humiliation, and about self-acceptance in the light of the Bible.

The teachings of Jesus Christ, the experience of Orthodoxy and Christianity, prayer, and the life of the Church lead you along this spiritual path to the salvation of the soul and to an understanding of what love is.

If you seek the Orthodox faith, yearn for the kingdom of heaven and paradise in your heart, want to restore joy and peace, let this word strengthen you.

All names of God lead to one thing: God is love, and God calls you His own.
