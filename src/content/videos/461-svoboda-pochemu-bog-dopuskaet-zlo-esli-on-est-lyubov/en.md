My child, you think freedom means doing whatever you want. But I speak to you as a Father. Freedom in the spirit is the ability to be who you were created to be—light and love. Freedom of choice is given to you not as a mistake, but as a risk of love. Love cannot be forced. It can only be chosen. Therefore, God does not cancel freedom, even when a person is hurt. He enters into freedom and transforms evil from within through those who have chosen love.

When you ask why God allows evil, hear the voice of the Creator in the silence. God is love, and God is near. God hears prayer. Prayer is not a distant cry, but the breath and presence of God. The living God speaks to you through the Bible, through the Gospel, through the image of Jesus Christ. Jesus showed that the Kingdom of Heaven is within, that salvation is the return of the memory of the Son, that forgiveness and grace are stronger than guilt.

The slavery of fear serves out of fear. The Son serves out of love. A friend serves out of unity. This is how faith grows, this is how the power of faith strengthens, this is how Christianity comes alive through the heart, not through rules. Orthodoxy of the heart, the Church as family, preaching as consolation—all this leads to one thing: liberation from fear and freedom from fear. This is spiritual awakening.

Let the love of the Creator become your path. Let peace in your heart become your home. Let hope become light. The light of Christ is already within you. Life with God begins within, where love lives, where Jesus breathes, where God abides, where love and faith and hope and salvation for every heart live.
