— How to be yourself without fear of rejection
— ​​Why spiritual awakening begins not with strength, but with honesty
— How faith, mindfulness, and bare-faced Christianity heal the soul

This self-development is not about “becoming better,” but about “being yourself.” This is soul healing for those tired of pretending.

Watch to the end—and you will hear words that will set you free.
