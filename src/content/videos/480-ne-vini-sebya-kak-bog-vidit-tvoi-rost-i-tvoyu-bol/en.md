This message is God’s living voice to a weary heart accustomed to blaming itself for every mistake. If you are familiar with guilt, the difficulty of self-acceptance, and the question of how to love and forgive yourself, listen to how God’s love looks at you.

The Lord says you are not a list of failures. Jesus Christ sees not only wounds but also the growth already occurring deep within. God is love, and this love brings healing to the soul, quiet faith, and inner light.

This is about forgiveness, how to stop blaming yourself, how to let go of the past through prayer and the word preserved in the Bible and the Church. This Christian sermon in the spirit of Orthodoxy is about what love truly is, about loving yourself without pride, about the self-development of the soul, and about self-confidence born of trust in Christ and Jesus.

About the motivation to rise after a fall, about the psychology of guilt, and about the silence of the heart, where prayer replaces anxiety, and the sermons of the Gospel lead to Christianity as a living encounter. Look at yourself through His eyes—and you will see how much beauty He has already created in you.
