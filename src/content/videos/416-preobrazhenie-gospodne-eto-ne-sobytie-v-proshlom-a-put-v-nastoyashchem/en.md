You think the Transfiguration is an event that happened long ago. That it’s just history. That it’s a testimony for those who followed Christ. And that it has nothing to do with you.

But this is what I tell you today: the Transfiguration is not just history. It’s a prophecy. It’s a revelation. It’s an invitation.

This video shows what really happened on the mountain:
Christ didn’t change. He showed who He always was. He didn’t become the light—He revealed the light that was within Him from the very beginning. The disciples didn’t see a miracle—they saw the reality hidden beneath ordinary flesh. The reality that waits to be revealed.

What this means for you now:
You, too, are called to the Transfiguration. Not to become someone else. But to reveal who you truly are. You don’t have to become the light—you have to let the light that’s already within you shine.

What this video is about: — The Transfiguration is not an event in the past. This is the path in the present—“Don’t become a saint—stop hiding the light”—Every person is called to Transfiguration, not just saints—How to become more transparent to love every day—Transform—not into someone else, but into your true self

Every day you can allow the light to pass through you. Every day you can become more transparent to love. Every day you can be transformed—not into someone else, but into your true self.

This is Transfiguration. Not when you become a saint. But when you stop hiding the light
