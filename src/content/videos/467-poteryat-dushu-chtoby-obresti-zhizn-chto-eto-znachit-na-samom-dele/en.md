You think that losing your soul means destroying yourself. But I tell you: it means ceasing to be the center of your universe.

My child, you fear losing your soul, but I want to return your soul to you in its purity. Your soul is depleted because the ego has made you a slave to your own security. You’ve built walls, you serve an idol, and at the center remains an emptiness.

Give Me your ego. Give Me your fear. Bring love where it’s dry. For God’s love does not kill—love uplifts. My grace is greater than your guilt. Hear God’s voice quietly within: God hears your every breath before it becomes a word.

What the world calls self-development and awareness, what it calls spirituality and spiritual development, spiritual awakening and inner transformation—all this is a longing for Me. When a pure soul awakens, peace comes to the heart. This is the salvation of the soul, and eternal life is already here—not as a reward, but as a return.

Open the Bible with your heart. Hear the Gospel not as a rule, but as the Word of God spoken to you. Jesus speaks there. Jesus Christ breathes there. Living faith dwells there.

Orthodoxy is with the heart. Orthodox prayer is like a quiet conversation at home. Christianity is love. The Church is like a family.

Let prayer become breathing. Let repentance become a return. Let faith become trust. Let hope become peace—where God is within, where God is near, where the Lord holds your hand.

God is the Father. God is near.

You will not disappear.

For the first time, you will become real.
