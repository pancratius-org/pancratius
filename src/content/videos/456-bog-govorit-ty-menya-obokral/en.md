You think I am far away. You hid Me in the heavens, but I am in your clay and breath.

My child, you made Me an icon, but I want to be a brother. You hid Me in gold and books, in the sky and the future, but I remained close. God is near. Not far. God is within. God is within you. God is within us.

I am not in gold, I am in your clay. I am in your breath, in your tears, in your weariness. Let prayer not be a cry into the distance, but a quiet conversation at home. God hears every breath before words.

What the world calls spirituality and awareness, what it calls inner transformation and spiritual transformation, what it calls the transformation of consciousness and the journey into oneself—all this is when your consciousness yearns for Me. This is spiritual awakening. Not an external miracle, but peace in the heart. Not noise, but light within.

Open the Bible with your heart. Hear the Gospel not as a rule, but as the word of God spoken to you. Jesus speaks there. Jesus Christ breathes there. Living faith dwells there. Orthodoxy through the heart, Christianity through love, the Church as a family—all living things lead to a meeting where faith becomes trust, where hope becomes peace, where love becomes home, where the soul remembers who it is.

I am not distant. I am the living and near Father. I am within you. Restore My nearness. I have always been here.
