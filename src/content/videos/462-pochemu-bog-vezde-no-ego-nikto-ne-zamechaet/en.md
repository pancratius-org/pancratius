A fish doesn’t know what water is. So, too, man doesn’t know what God is. He lives in Him, breathes Him, but doesn’t notice.

This parable about the fish and water is the creator’s answer to how to feel God and how to hear God in the silence between thoughts, in breathing, in the heartbeat.

Many ask where God lives and continue to search for God outside of themselves. But the truth is that God is within us, God is near, God is with you, and God is in you. You live in Him. This is the living presence of God. This kingdom of God is within you. This kingdom is within, open as the home of the soul.

This word of God sounds like the voice of God, like Jesus speaking in the heart, like a prayer without words, like the spiritual music of silence.

This is the awakening of the soul. This is a spiritual awakening. This is awakening. This is faith and love that have become home and life.

Stop. Be silent. Feel. This is home. This is life. This is love.
