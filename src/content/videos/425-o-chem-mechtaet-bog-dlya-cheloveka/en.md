You ask, “Lord, what do You dream of for me?”
You think He dreams of making you more successful. Stronger. Richer. Happier. But all of these are just circumstances. His dream is much deeper.

This video contains the answer that turns everything you thought about God upside down:
He dreams that you will one day stop being afraid to be loved. That you will stop earning what has always been His gift. That love will become as natural to you as breathing.

What God really dreams of:
- Not great temples.
- Not living hearts.
- Not great religion.
- Not children who know their Father.
- Not through fear.
- Not through a sense of duty.
- Not through joy.
- Not through coercion.
- Through freedom.

His Kingdom does not come by thunder. Not by force. Not by the victory of some over others. It grows in every heart that chooses love.

And one day you will see: His dream was never to create a world where people fear Him. His dream is a world where people know they are not strangers. One Source. One Life. One Love.

And then you will understand: this was never just His dream. It was always the deepest dream of your own heart.
