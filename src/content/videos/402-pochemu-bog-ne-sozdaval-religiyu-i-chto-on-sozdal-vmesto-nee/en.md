No. People built walls. Invented rules. Wrote dogmas. Divided the world into “right” and “wrong.” They called My home a system. And God inaccessible.

But I didn’t create religion. I created freedom.

This video contains a direct word from Jesus Christ that turns the very concept of faith upside down:
— Religion seeks God. I seek you.
— Religion builds walls. I build the heart.
— Religion gives answers. I give Myself.

What is the real Church? Not a building. Not an organization. Not a set of rules. The Church is those in whom I live. Those who hear Me. Those who love without judgment. Those who are not afraid to be weak. Those in whose eyes you see not a judge, but Me.

You don’t have to choose between religions. You must choose Me. But I am not within walls. I am within you.

This is the Church. This is freedom.
