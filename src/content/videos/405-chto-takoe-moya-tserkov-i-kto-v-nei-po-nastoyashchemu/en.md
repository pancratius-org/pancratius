You think the Church is a building, a teaching, or a rite.
But Christ says otherwise. His Church is not walls. Not rituals. Not an organization. It is a living organism, of which He is the Head, and each believer is a cell in His body.

This video contains a direct word from Jesus Christ that turns the very concept of the Church upside down:

1. Whose Church is this? Christ and the Father are one. Do not seek division where there is none. The Church is the place where He acts, lives, and meets with people.
2. Was there a Church before Christ? Yes. Abraham, Moses, the prophets—they were the Church of expectation. The Church is not a project of the New Testament, but God’s eternal intention: to be with people.
3. Who is inside and who is outside? Not the one who knows the right words. But the one who is united with Him in spirit. A sinner who repents in silence is inside. A priest without love can be outside.
4. Does He excommunicate? Only those who have separated themselves through the constant rejection of love. And even then, not forever. The door is always open.
5. What is the Church, really? It is the air His Presence breathes. Don’t look for a place. Look for the Presence. Where He is, there is the Church. And He is not within the walls. He is within you.

You can be outside the building and yet within Him. You can be within the building and yet be far from Him. This is the mystery of His Church. The mystery you carry within you.
