You ask why the one you loved more than life has passed away, and this question is so painful and sorrowful. This is a word from God for a grieving heart, a quiet conversation with God about the death of a loved one, about the soul, and about life after death.

There are no accusations here. God says He didn’t take, but accepted. Death came not as His decision, but as a consequence of a finite world, in which the body cannot last forever. He entered into this pain to be with the departed and with you.

If you are experiencing loss and don’t know how to move on, how to bear the emptiness and cope with grief, listen to these words of comfort. Your love has not disappeared. The one you love is not lost; they are at home, alive in eternity, in the hands of the Creator. And you are left with hope and the path of faith.

Prayer addressed to God in sorrow, reading the Bible and the Gospels, and meditating on God’s word help you hear God’s voice. The Orthodox faith, Christianity, and the wisdom of the Church remind us of the resurrection, the salvation of the soul, the encounter with God, and the Kingdom of God, where love is stronger than death.

You can cry, you can scream, you can ask questions. God is not afraid of your tears or your anger. He is only afraid that you will close yourself off. This is a message about God’s presence near, about grace, about forgiveness, and about the meaning of life, which sustains the soul in the darkest days and restores peace to the heart.

Stay here, listen to the end, and let this answer from the Creator touch you.
