This film is a conversation about the meaning of human life. I did not create you because I was lonely or because I needed a servant. Love, by its very nature, desires to be shared. You are not a project. You are a desire. You are not a function. You are a presence. You are the purpose of creation.

If you ask questions about who I am and why I live, if you are searching for yourself and a spiritual quest, listen: you haven’t lost your image. You’ve lost the awareness of your image. Exile isn’t being kicked out. It’s when you no longer feel at home. You became an exile not because I closed the door, but because you stopped seeing yourself through My eyes.

This video presents a path to God without fear or the religion of effort. Not to become better. Not to correct oneself. Just to open the door. Faith in God as trust, prayer as a meeting, the soul as a home where peace of mind returns. Here, God is near, here, God’s love and God is love guide you through the Bible and the Gospel—in living Christianity and Orthodoxy. Here, Jesus Christ speaks quietly: you are not a mistake. You are desired. And hope is revived in the heart.
