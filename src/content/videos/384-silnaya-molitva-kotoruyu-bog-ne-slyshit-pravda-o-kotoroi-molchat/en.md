Why doesn’t God answer powerful prayer as a person asks?

Because the power of prayer is not measured by human effort.
You call something powerfully felt a powerful prayer.
God sees deeper.

Sometimes you ask loudly—but out of fear.
Sometimes with tears—but you want your own safety, not His will.
Sometimes you ask for good—but your desire is mingled with pride.

God hears every prayer.
But hearing doesn’t mean obeying orders.
A father doesn’t give a child everything he demands.
Not because He doesn’t love him. But because He loves him more than you understand.

His love doesn’t always match your expectations.
But it never ceases to be love.
