You think age is the end. But I tell you: it is a new beginning. You can start building yourself at any age. Not a house. Not a career. But yourself.

This video shows the most important construction:
You shouldn’t build something that requires a lot of effort. You should build something that requires the heart. And while you’re alive, you can grow. Not in your body, but in your soul. And this is the most important construction.

If you’re looking for the meaning of life and why I live, listen: you don’t need a young body to start how to start a new life. You need a living heart. To forgive. To love in a new way. To see beauty in the ordinary. To pray with your heart. God’s love doesn’t ask for a passport—It asks, “Are you ready to grow?”

This is spiritual awakening—when prayer becomes not a request to regain youth, but an encounter with the One who is building you from within. God is love, and He is building you even now
