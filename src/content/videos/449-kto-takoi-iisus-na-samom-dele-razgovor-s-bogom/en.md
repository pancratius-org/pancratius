You will hear what it means to break the cross, how to remove guilt, what it means to purify the heart, and how to distinguish a voice that brings peace from one that promises power without peace. This is a word about intimacy, in which God is closer than breath, about faith as trust, about prayer as silence, where the soul meets the Father.

If you are tormented by division, if you seek the path to God through the pain of the world, if your soul thirsts for spiritual awakening, repentance, and the living presence of the Holy Spirit—stay and listen to the end. Let the Bible and the Gospel reveal themselves to you as a message of unity, where the path to God is open to every heart.

Let this word become a bridge where Christianity and Islam, prayer and silence, soul and eternity meet in the one love of the Father.
