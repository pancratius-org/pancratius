You live with the feeling that love must be earned.
Give—and wait for appreciation. Show—and check if it’s enough.
You prove your love with actions, words, patience, sacrifice.
And you fear: if you stop proving, people will stop loving you.

This is God’s message for those who are tired of earning.
Self-love and self-acceptance—not through merit, but through freedom.
Permission to love without accountability. Simply love—like light.

This is faith without stress. Christianity without proof.
Awareness: your love is not your work, but His presence within you.
This is healing the soul from the fear of “I don’t love enough.”

God’s words today: you do not create love. You simply give it space.
This self-development is not through “becoming better,” but through “relaxing and allowing.”
God doesn’t require certificates. His love is already sufficient.

Don’t prove yourself. Just be in love.
It transforms everything it touches.
