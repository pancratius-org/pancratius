Rise, My child, I do not want you to crawl on your knees. I call you to stand as a son, not as a slave.

My child, you think that prayer is a slave’s cry, that repentance is humiliation, that forgiveness must be earned through fear. But hear God’s voice quietly within. I speak to you as a Father, not as a judge.

Humility is not humiliation. Humility and pride are opposites. Pride hides and flees, but humility comes close and looks into the eyes of love. You are no less because I am greater. You are great because you are the son of God, born of love.

Ask like a son. Let the prayer of repentance not be a distant cry, but a quiet word at home. God hears the whisper of the heart before words. God is love, and God’s love does not humiliate, but uplifts. God’s mercy is broader than your guilt. Grace is already revealed to you.

For a soul weary of fear, this is the salvation of the soul. This is living faith, when faith becomes trust, when hope becomes peace, when love becomes home, when peace is born in the heart.

Orthodoxy through the heart, Orthodox faith through love, Christianity as the path of the son, the Church as family—all living things lead to the Father. Open the Bible with your heart. Hear the Gospel not as a rule, but as the word of God spoken to you. Jesus speaks there. Jesus Christ breathes there.

Remain in silence. Come. I am already waiting for you with an embrace.
