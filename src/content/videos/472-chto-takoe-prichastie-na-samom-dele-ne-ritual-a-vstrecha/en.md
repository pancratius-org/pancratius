You think Communion is only bread and wine. Only what happens in church. Only a ritual.

But I tell you: you can partake of Me in everything.

This video contains the truth that breaks down the last wall between the “sacred” and the “ordinary”:
Communion is not only bread. It is all of life. If you know how to see.

You can partake of Me in a look. In silence. In a person. In pain. In circumstances. Even in what seems like sin and ego—if you know how to discern.

What this video is about: - Why Communion is not a ritual, but an encounter - How to recognize God in everything without deifying creation itself - “I am in everything, but not everything is I”: the art of discernment - How your whole life becomes one continuous Communion

You receive Communion not to dissolve. But to see. Not to become everything. But to recognize Me in everything.

Learn to see. Learn to discern. And then not just bread—but every moment of your life—will become Communion. Not as a ritual. As an encounter. As love.
