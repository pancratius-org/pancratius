You do not go to hell or heaven after death. You live in them right now—in every look, in every word, in every movement of your heart. Hell is not geography. It is a state of separation from God. Heaven is not a place. It is a state of oneness with Him.

Your gaze creates your world. If you look with condemnation, you are in hell. If with love, you are in heaven. And you can change them not after death. Right now. Simply by changing your gaze.

This revelation is a spiritual awakening that is not spoken of. Who will go to heaven? The one who chooses love. Right now.
