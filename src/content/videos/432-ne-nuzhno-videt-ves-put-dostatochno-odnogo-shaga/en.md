You cry out, “Lord, what should I do?!” And I answer, “Be with Me. I will show you the rest.”

You think you need to see the entire path before taking the first step. But the road opens not to those who see everything, but to those who walk.

This video tells you what to do when you don’t know what to do:
Don’t panic. Don’t look for an answer—look for Me. Don’t plan the entire path—take one step. And trust that I will show you the next one when you’re ready.

Trusting God isn’t “understanding the plan.” It’s allowing the Guide to lead. You are not alone. You are not lost. You are not without a path. You just don’t yet know how close I am.

God’s will is revealed in action, not in thought. As long as you stand and demand a map, you stand. But as soon as you take a step, the path appears beneath your feet. Faith in God isn’t confidence in the future. It’s confidence in the One who leads. And if you’re seeking spiritual awakening, it begins right here: not “I know what will happen,” but “I know Who is with me.”

Take a step. The first one. The rest is My concern. The path opens not to those who see everything, but to those who trust.
