You may feel invisible. You speak, and they don’t hear you. You try, and they don’t appreciate you. People leave. Promises fall apart. You’re left alone.

But here’s what you forget in this pain: you are not lost.

God knows you. Not your reputation. Not your accomplishments. Not your mistakes. He knows your name—the one He named you by before you were born.

This video offers a direct word of comfort for anyone who feels rejected:
- When people leave, He remains
- When they reject, He accepts
- When they misunderstand, He understands

These aren’t just words. This is a reminder: You are not what others think you are. You are what God knows you are. And He doesn’t change His mind about you. Ever.

You are not alone. You just haven’t looked back yet. He is here. He has always been here. And He is not leaving.
