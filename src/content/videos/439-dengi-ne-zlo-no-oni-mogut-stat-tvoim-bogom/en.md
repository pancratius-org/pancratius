The meaning of life isn’t about how much you have. Money isn’t just pieces of paper. It’s the power you trust. It’s the protection you’re confident in. It’s the wall you hide behind when you’re afraid. The danger isn’t in money. It’s in the fact that you stop needing Me.

This video presents the ultimate test:
Wealth is not a death sentence. It’s a test. A test of what you’re willing to give. A test of what you’re willing to trust Me more than your wallet. The rich can enter the Kingdom if they learn to be poor before Me—when God becomes first again, and everything else is just a resource you’re willing to give at My word. If you’re looking for how to find yourself, why I live, and why all this—hear this: I’m not asking you to throw everything away. I’m asking you to give Me first place in your heart. This is spiritual awakening—when faith in God becomes trust, prayer becomes a meeting, and the soul finds peace. Where God’s love and God is love lead you along the path to God—through the Bible and the Gospel, in living Christianity and Orthodoxy. Where Jesus Christ is near, and hope is revived in the heart.
