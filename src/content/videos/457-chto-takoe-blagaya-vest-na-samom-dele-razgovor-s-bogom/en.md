In this conversation, we explore how a simple change from the word “is” to “will” has impacted our perception of eternal life. We often become accustomed to rules, forgetting the essence, and lose our living connection with God. The author reveals what was lost in the process of word transmission and how this has distorted our spiritual quest.

This is an opportunity to rethink familiar concepts and return to what truly matters. If you feel that the form of faith has become more important than the content itself, this conversation will help you find the path to true salvation. We don’t simply discuss dogma; we seek the meaning of life in silence and sincerity.
