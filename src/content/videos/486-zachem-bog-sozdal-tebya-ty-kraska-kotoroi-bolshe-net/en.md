Why did God create you? The question that underlies the entire search for the meaning of life. The answer here isn’t dogma, but a personal word: He didn’t need you—He wanted you.

If you’ve been searching for the meaning of your life, asking why God created people and why He created you specifically—this conversation is about what was before the world began. About love that isn’t hunger, but abundance. About a river that flows not because the earth is dry, but because it has too much water.

Who is God in this story? Not a distant force or a warden, but a Source with desire and a face. The entire Gospel speaks of this—from Adam and Eve to the prophets. There’s no debate about religion here; there’s a quiet examination of the Bible and God’s word spoken about you.

For those accustomed to seeking spirituality in esotericism and practices, something else is offered: not a technique, but an encounter. Faith in God begins not with effort, but with being called by name. And then faith ceases to be an obligation.

This isn’t a priest’s sermon from the pulpit, nor are these formulaic Christian sermons. This is a conversation about the Kingdom of Heaven within, about grace that cannot be earned, about salvation as a return home, about repentance that doesn’t humiliate, but liberates. There is no path through fear here—only through love.

There is also something rarely spoken about: you were created to reflect beauty in a way no one else can. Without you, the universe will be left with a void of that color that was uniquely yours.

Awakening is not noise. Spiritual awakening comes in silence, in prayer, in self-honesty. This is the same at the core of Christianity, the depth of Orthodoxy, and every tradition where people sought not power, but intimacy.

Jesus Christ speaks simply: love has a face, and it is turned toward you.

This channel is not a church in the sense of a building, nor is it a philosophy of life for consolation. This is a place where people ask the question “how to find yourself” and hear in response: you are not an accident.

Wisdom here isn’t in rules, but in recognition. Spiritual development isn’t a ladder upward, but a movement inward. Personal growth without this encounter often remains a pretty shell.

If you know the feeling that your place at the table is empty, listen to the end. Paradise begins not afterward, but within.

God’s Word doesn’t require you to first become a different person. Answer with your heart. Peace be with you.
