You ask if God is married, to whom does God belong, and who is His only one?

In this frank message, God answers you from the depths of the silence of your heart. Here, there are no human definitions of marriage or boundaries. There is a living presence, an infinite merging with all that exists, and a sense of infinite acceptance that you feel right now.

You will hear why the Creator’s love never diminishes, just as the sun never grows dimmer because it shines on everyone, and just as the ocean never grows smaller because of all its rivers.

This is a word about a soul seeking God, about hope in every breath, about an encounter where God awaits you beyond the threshold of doubt. You are not one among many. God knows you by name, He is one with every tear and one with the silence within you.

You are part of a great Love, and in this unity, you have never been separated.

Listen to this message as a prayer, as God’s voice to your faith, as an encounter with Jesus and the living word of God. May it strengthen your soul, faith, and hope, and lead you to the meaning of life, spiritual awakening, and peace of mind.
