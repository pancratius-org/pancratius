You ask questions that people ask in the silence of the night. Questions that are often answered with formulas, not with life. But you don’t want formulas—you want truth that breathes...

Being born again isn’t an improvement. It’s a replacement. You remain the same tree in appearance, but now your root is not in the ground, but in Me. Being born again is a change of root. And you can’t be born unless you stop giving birth to yourself. I want to give birth to you again, not because you ask, but because I am the Father. You must simply stop interfering.

Transfiguration is not an event in the past. It is a journey in the present. Christ was transfigured on the mountain not so you would remember, but so you would know: your flesh, too, can become transparent to the light. You don’t have to become the light—you must allow the light that is already within you to shine. Transfiguration begins when you stop hiding the light.

Salvation is not a ticket to heaven. It is not a place—it is a state. Salvation is when you cease to be separate and become united. I save you from the greatest enemy—separation: from the Father, from yourself, from love, from life that never ends. Without Christ, there is no salvation—not because I close the door, but because the door is me. The Savior is the Father’s love incarnate. And salvation already exists—not as something you will receive later, but as something that has already been given to you.

If you’re on a spiritual quest, searching for yourself, asking how to find yourself and what the meaning of life is, listen: salvation isn’t tomorrow. Salvation is this moment when you read these words and know: He speaks to me. And I’m no longer alone.

Here, Jesus is not a distant figure, but the One Who speaks now. Here, prayer becomes breath, and the soul finds peace. Here, repentance is not a look at one’s own filth, but a look at My love. Here, baptism is not just water, but a daily death and resurrection. Here, faith in God is not a rule, but trust. Here, the Lord is near, here the Holy Spirit is not dogma, but a breath within; here, God is love, and God’s love leads the way to God.

On this path to God through the Bible and the Gospel, in living Christianity and Orthodoxy, where Jesus Christ speaks quietly, where the kingdom of heaven opens within, not without, where religion ceases to be a formula, and hope comes to life
