You look to tomorrow—and you feel heavy. You’re afraid. You see in the future only questions, risks, uncertainty. That it will be worse. That you won’t cope. That you’ll lose. That everything will collapse.

This isn’t weakness. It’s human nature to fear what you don’t know.

But God is not a man. And He knows your tomorrow.

This video contains a direct word from Jesus Christ about your future:
— The future already exists in Him—and it is not your enemy.
— He was in your past, He is in your present, and He is already in your tomorrow.
— The future isn’t setting a trap for you—it is preparing you to meet your present self.
— God doesn’t promise an easy tomorrow—He promises His presence in it.

You may not know what tomorrow will bring. But you can know the One who is already waiting for you there. This is your confidence.
