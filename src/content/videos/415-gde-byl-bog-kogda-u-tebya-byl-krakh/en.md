You look at the rubble. Plans have collapsed, hopes have been dashed, and it seems like God is silent. But that’s not true. He was in your wreckage—not to save your structures, but to save you.

This video is a message for those who stand in the midst of chaos and see no way out. Discover what God really does when your familiar world collapses:

1. How He rebuilds you from your wounds.
2. Why He removes false supports from your life.
3. What gift He is preparing for you right in the center of darkness.

What you consider the end may be the beginning of your true home, one that cannot be destroyed.
