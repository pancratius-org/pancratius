You pray every day. You cry, you wait, and you ask God to bring your son or daughter back, but they drift further and further away. In this letter, the Father responds to a mother who is tired of bearing what cannot be changed on her own.

Prayer, powerful prayer, and prayer for her son here become not an attempt to hold on to the child, but trust in God: you sow, He will water. God hears every tear and every unspoken word. Love for children doesn’t disappear when a mother stops holding them—it turns into trust.

Listen to the end to hear the answer: don’t drag them away, but give them into the hands of the one who loves them more and will never abandon them.
