Have you ever asked yourself: Why am I living if I’m of no use?

This message from God isn’t a Christian sermon from a pulpit, but a quiet voice that speaks directly to your heart. This video delivers a living message about how your worth isn’t measured by your results. God tells you what’s rarely said: you weren’t created to be useful. You’re not a tool or a machine. You are someone who is loved.

We’re used to measuring ourselves by success, and that creates loneliness. Self-esteem plummets when we compare ourselves to others, and our purpose seems alien and unattainable. But God’s message doesn’t require you to be productive. God’s plan isn’t for you to burn out on someone else’s expectations.

Self-acceptance begins not with strength, but with honesty. Sometimes the greatest benefit you bring to the world is your vulnerability, your sincerity, your ability to ask for help. The strength of your faith is revealed in your weakness. In your brokenness, a new temple is being built.

Repentance is not self-flagellation. It is a homecoming. It is when you stop being “someone” and allow God to love you as you are. Unconditional love does not expect an accountability from you.

If you are struggling right now, if you are tired of searching for the meaning of life, watch this to the end. There are no demands or accusations here. There is only a quiet prayer and a whisper: rest. You are My breath, clothed in form.

In this message, God speaks to you not as an instrument, but as someone He loves. Healing of the soul begins where you stop fighting with yourself.

This video is for those seeking a spiritual path, who are tired of empty promises and want to hear God’s word without intermediaries. For those who want to understand how to love themselves without succumbing to pride. For those who are going through a time of change and are looking for hope.

Spiritual awakening rarely looks like an explosion. More often, it is one honest sigh. One question. One phrase spoken in silence: “I made no mistake in creating you.”

This is not a reward for merit, but love already given to you. Faith in God is not something you must earn. A philosophy of life that begins with this changes forever. If you seek the meaning of life, stop and hear these words. The soul knows this conversation.
