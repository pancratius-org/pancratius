You think hell is a place where you can be “sent” or “locked away.” But I tell you: hell is a state of oblivion. A thick fog of fear, guilt, separation, and pain that you’ve created around yourself, believing the lie that you’re alone and have to pay for something.

In this video, we’ll explore what heaven and hell really are:

Every time you felt hopeless, when anger burned you from within, when loneliness became unbearable—that was the very “hell” that the mind creates.

And heaven... Heaven isn’t golden gates in the sky. Heaven is awakening. That moment when you suddenly realize: “I am not this pain, I am not this fear, I am not this small, limited personality.”

Spiritual awakening is the realization of your true oneness with God. The silence that comes when all worries fade away, and you simply feel God’s infinite love permeating every cell of your being.

If you ask what heaven and hell are, the answer isn’t in geography. The answer is in state. Hell is forgetting who you are. Heaven is remembering.

You’re not being punished. You simply forgot. And once you remember, the fog will clear, and you’ll understand: you were never in hell. You were always at home, in My arms
