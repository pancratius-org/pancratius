You ask how to confess properly and how to begin a conversation with God. This message is the Creator’s answer to your soul.

Confession is not a report to a judge or a to-do list for the church. Confession is an embrace, a living encounter where prayer becomes breath, and repentance becomes a homecoming.

Here we speak of faith without fear, of Orthodoxy of the heart, of the sacrament of repentance as mercy. You will hear why God looks not at sin but at wounds, how God’s voice resounds in silence, and how grace heals the soul.

This word comes from Holy Scripture and the New Testament, from the depths of the Gospel, where Jesus Christ meets people not with reproach, but with love.

If your soul is tired of guilt and shame, if you seek salvation and forgiveness, if you want to understand the meaning of prayer to God and feel the presence of the Father—stay in this silence. Begin simply with presence and say from your heart that you are struggling and you desire to be closer.

May this Christian teaching strengthen your faith in God, bring peace to your heart, and remind you that you are already loved and forgiven.
