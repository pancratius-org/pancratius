This question is one of the most painful. It torments those who suffer and those who see others suffer.
You think, “If I’m sick, it means I’ve sinned. It means God is punishing me.” And this thought brings not only pain but also guilt. You bear a double burden: the illness itself and the feeling that you deserved it.

This video gives the answer without any pretense. Because the truth sets you free.

Illness is not a punishment. It is not a retribution for sin. Yes, there are illnesses that are the result of lifestyle choices. Yes, there are illnesses that come from unforgiven grievances and a closed heart. But illness itself is not a sentence. Not a test. Not revenge.

What this video is about: - Why illness is not a punishment, but a part of a world where not everything is yet renewed - The story of the blind man about whom the disciples asked: “Who sinned—this man or his parents?” - What God does with your illness: He doesn’t send you, but enters into it - Why suffering can become a meeting place, not a sentence

God doesn’t wish for you to suffer. But when it comes, He walks with you through it. Illness isn’t evidence of your guilt. It’s evidence that you live in a world where not everything is yet new.

And He didn’t come to condemn you through illness. He came to walk through it with you—and lead you to a life stronger than death.
