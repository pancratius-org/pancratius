This word is about God’s will and free will, about human destiny and the meaning of life.

God speaks to you in silence: I do not decide your fate, I entrust it to you. Not because I have abandoned you, but because love does not compel. Thus, a living faith, not a ritualistic one, is revealed—a faith where religion becomes an encounter, and prayer a conversation.

Here resounds the spirit of the Bible and the New Testament, the wisdom of the Gospel and the experience of the Church, the voice of Orthodoxy and all Christianity. Jesus and Jesus Christ reveal the Kingdom of Heaven within, the priest and the sermon remind us of repentance, the Our Father and powerful prayer, morning prayers and evening prayers, prayer to God and daily prayer strengthen hope. A spiritual beacon for life, where everything is God’s will, but the decision is yours. You decide your fate, but you are not alone—God is near, and love supports you even when you make mistakes.
