The death of the ego isn’t the end of you. It’s the end of the illusion about you.

If you’re looking for how to find yourself and asking “who am I?”—listen: you’re waking up. You’re becoming who you were meant to be. You’re not losing yourself—you’re finally allowing the real you to live. This isn’t a loss. It’s a gain.

This is spiritual awakening—when, through self-searching and questioning the purpose of all this, you come to faith in God, where God is near, where God’s love and God is love lead you along the path to God—through the Bible and the Gospel, in living Christianity and Orthodoxy. Where Jesus Christ is near, prayer becomes a meeting, and the soul finds peace and hope
