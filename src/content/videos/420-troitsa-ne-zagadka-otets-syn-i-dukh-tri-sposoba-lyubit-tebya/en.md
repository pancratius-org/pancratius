You think that the Father, Son, and Spirit are three different Gods. Or three masks. Or three hypostases that are difficult to understand. People have been arguing about this for centuries.

But this is what I tell you today: They are not three. They are one. Revealed in different ways so that you can contain Me.

This video contains a revelation that resolves the age-old debate:

The Father is the plan. It is Me before you knew Me. It is the love that conceived you before you were born. It is the source. It is the home.

The Son is the way. It is when I became visible. When I entered your reality, shared your pain and your hope. It is My face turned to you. It is the Bridge. It is the Embrace.

The Holy Spirit is the breath. It is when I live within you. When you feel Me, even if you cannot see Me. When you pray, even if you don’t know the words, it is life breathing through you.

They are not three. They are one action of Love in three forms:
The Father—Love that conceives you. The Son—Love that comes to you. The Spirit—Love that lives within you.

And when you know this, you stop arguing about which of them is “more important.” They are all one. One desire: to know that you are loved.
