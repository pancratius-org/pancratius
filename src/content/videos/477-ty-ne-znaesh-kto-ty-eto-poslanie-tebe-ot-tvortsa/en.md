When old definitions, roles, mistakes, and fears no longer answer the question of your identity, you can pause in silence and hear the most important thing: you are not lost.

In this message, the Creator reminds you: you are not your mistakes or your fears. You are loved, already found, and you can simply be. Dwell in this “I don’t know” without rushing and allow your heart to hear the voice of love.
