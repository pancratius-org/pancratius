You are the only one in the world. And this is not pride. It is the truth...

I did not create you from a template. You are the only copy of My design. And therein lies your holiness. You are not a copy or a duplicate. You are not mass-produced. You are unique.

Don’t be afraid of being unique. Be afraid of giving up your uniqueness. Of trying to be like everyone else. Of hiding your unique light behind someone else’s mask. Of trading yourself for someone else’s approval.

If you are going through a self-discovery and spiritual quest, if you ask why I live and who I am, if the meaning of life is elusive, and the path to finding yourself seems endless—listen: you are not looking for a new version of yourself. You are returning to your uniqueness. Simply being yourself is how to be yourself.

How can you give up on the opinions of others? Not through effort or armor. When you see yourself through the Father’s eyes, the gaze of others loses its power. Then self-esteem ceases to depend on applause, self-worth and self-acceptance become not an idea, but an experience, and self-confidence is born not from victories, but from the truth about who you were created to be.

How to love yourself? Not through achievements—by returning home. You don’t have to earn your uniqueness. You are already chosen by God. Not for glory. For service. Not to ascend, but to be who you were created to be. And that is enough.

This is spiritual awakening—when faith in God becomes not a rule, but a perspective. Where God is love, where God’s love and God is near, where Jesus Christ quietly speaks: go with your uniqueness and give it to no one. Where the Bible and the Gospel read like a letter to you, where Christianity and Orthodoxy are not walls but a living path to God, where prayer brings the soul home, peace becomes silence, and hope is revived.
