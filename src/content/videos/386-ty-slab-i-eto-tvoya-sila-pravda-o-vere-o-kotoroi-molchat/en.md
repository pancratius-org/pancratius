God says: Your weakness is not a curse, but a door to faith.

Can a weak person have true faith? Yes.
Often, it is the weak who are closer to true faith if they don’t hide their weakness from God.

The strong rely on themselves. The intelligent rely on their wits. The rich rely on their wealth.
The weak, if they are honest, quickly understand: “I am not the source.”
This becomes the door to faith.

But weakness in itself does not save. It can embitter, make one envious.
Weakness becomes a place of faith only when offered to God.
Then His strength is perfected in weakness.

Not because weakness is good. Because in it, man stops pretending to be God.

This is true Christianity. This is the spirituality Jesus Christ spoke of.
The Bible is full of examples: God chooses the weak. Not to humiliate them, but to demonstrate His love.
Your humility before Him is not weakness. It is the most powerful prayer.
