You wait for Me in the clouds. But I stand next to you—and you don’t see Me...

This is a film-discussion about the Second Coming of Christ and the judgment of God. You fear judgment—but My judgment is not what you fear. My judgment is not punishment. It is healing. Judgment is stripping to heal. I am the judge who became the defendant. My judgment is the Cross, on which I said, “Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.”

You will not be judged by your deeds—deeds cannot justify you. You will be judged by your love—by whether you have accepted My love. I am not a judge waiting to condemn. I am a judge waiting to embrace.

And the Second Coming has already begun. Not as an event that will happen one day, but as a process that is always happening. I am not the God of tomorrow. I am the God of today, this minute, this breath. The greatest tragedy is to fail to notice My presence here and now, because our eyes have become accustomed to looking for Me there, then, in another form.

I have already come. And I always come. And I will come again—but not as you are accustomed to expect. Not as a great judgment of the world, but as a meeting. Not as a final judgment, but as a heavenly court of love, where you are not condemned, but returned.

You are my coming. When you come to a person with love, I come. When you give him hope, I come. When you forgive, I come. When you are silent and listen, I come. My coming is not the end of the world. It is the beginning of a new world.

If you’re searching for yourself and a spiritual quest, if you’re asking who I am and why I live, listen: you’re not looking for a new version of yourself. You’re looking for the One who’s always been there. God is near—not tomorrow, but here.

Here, prayer becomes breath, and the Jesus Prayer is not a technique, but an encounter. Here, the Holy Spirit is not dogma, but the breath within. Here, repentance is not a look at one’s own filth, but a look at My love. Here, faith in God is not a rule, but trust. Here, forgiveness is not an effort, but a liberation. Here, spiritual awakening is not an achievement, but a recognition.

You didn’t lose the image. You lost the awareness of the image. And returning isn’t about becoming better. It’s about opening a door. On the path to God through the Bible and the Gospel, in living Christianity and Orthodoxy, where Jesus Christ quietly says: I am here. I have always been here. Where God’s love and God is love lead the soul home, where peace and hope come alive.
