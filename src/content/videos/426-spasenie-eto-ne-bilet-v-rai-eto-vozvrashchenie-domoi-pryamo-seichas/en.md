Salvation isn’t something that happens to you after death. It’s something that happens to you now. It’s when you wake up and realize that you’re already home. That you’re already in My arms.

This video explains what salvation really is:
Salvation isn’t the end of the road. It’s the beginning of a life in which you know you’re not alone. In which you know you’re loved. Salvation is when fear, guilt, and loneliness recede because you’ve remembered the most important thing: I am near. I have always been near.

You’re not saved when you go to heaven. You’re saved when you remember that you’re already in My arms. Right now. Here. Not after death.

This video is about returning home. About the presence of God within you. About the inner light that already exists within you. About how healing the soul begins not with effort, but with remembrance.

If you feel lonely, this message is for you. You are not alone. You have never been alone. This is your salvation
