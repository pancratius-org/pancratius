God says: it is not a feeling or a sacrifice. Love is a unity where two become one. God is love—and this is not a metaphor, but a reality. Spiritual love is deeper than emotions. God’s love leads you out of loneliness. You think you know what love is. But love is not an emotion. It is a new state of being. When I cease to be separate. When boundaries are erased. When your pain becomes Mine. This is a revelation about the nature of God that changes everything.
