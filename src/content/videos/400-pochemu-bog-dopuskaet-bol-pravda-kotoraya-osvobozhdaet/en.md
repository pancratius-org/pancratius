You ask, “Why is God silent when I cry out?” It’s a question without an easy answer. And anyone who says, “It’s God’s will,” isn’t telling the whole truth.

God doesn’t send pain. He enters into it. Not as a savior with ready-made answers. But as the One who sits beside you in your darkness.

When you lose meaning, He becomes meaning. And in the darkest night, His love is more real than any joy.

You won’t fully understand. But you can trust. Pain doesn’t have the final word. His light does.
