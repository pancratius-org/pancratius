You fear that closeness to God will take away your life, freedom, and dreams. This message is a direct answer, in which God speaks with the tenderness of the Heavenly Father: I came not to take away, but to fill.

Here is revealed how to find God not in withdrawal from the world, but in the most ordinary day—in the warmth of hands, in the silence between thoughts, in caring for children, in joy, in morning prayers and evening prayers.

You will learn that life with God is not a renunciation of earthly things, but an inner transformation, where God is near, God is with us, and God is within us.

This message is about God being love, about God’s love being alive in every moment, about the Lord and Heavenly Father being near, and about Jesus Christ, as the Son of God, revealing the Kingdom of Heaven within, as spoken of in the Holy Scriptures.

Through prayer and prayer to the Lord, through Orthodox prayer, through the power of prayer, the soul is healed, the fear of self-loathing subsides, and inner peace and strong faith in God are born.

God’s voice resounds softly: God loves you, God helps you, the Holy Spirit breathes within you.

This is a true spiritual awakening for everyone who seeks the path. Remain the most earthly of earthly people—and feel how eternity touches the mundane.
