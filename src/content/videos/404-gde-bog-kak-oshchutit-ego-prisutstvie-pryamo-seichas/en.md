God speaks to you today: “I am not hiding. I am waiting.”
You will not find Me in words. Not in rituals. Not in searching outside yourself.
I am in your breath. In the silence between thoughts.
When you stop trying, you will feel peace. As if someone is near. As if you are alone.
This is the meeting. I am already within you. And I have always been there.
Pause. Breathe. Notice.
