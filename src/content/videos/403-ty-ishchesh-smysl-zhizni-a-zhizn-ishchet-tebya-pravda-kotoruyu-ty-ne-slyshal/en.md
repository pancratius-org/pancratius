What is the meaning of life? Why do I live? God tells you today: your search for the meaning of life ends here. Jesus revealed: the Kingdom of God is within you. This revelation from God about the meaning of life and the search for self is a truth you haven’t heard.

You cycle through roles, paths, achievements. But meaning is not a formula. It is an encounter. Not in what you do. But in Who is with you.

Much is said about God. But to know Him means to find yourself. This is spiritual awakening: when you stop searching and begin to be.

This crisis of meaning is familiar to everyone. But the solution lies not in motivation. And not in philosophy. The solution lies in trust. You already matter. You are already part of a plan. Faith in God is not theory. It is the path to your purpose.

You will see: finding yourself is not a question of searching. It is a question of allowing. How to find your purpose? Stop asking “why” – start being. And then you will see: you haven’t found the meaning of life. You have become it.
