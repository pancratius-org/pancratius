If you feel like prayer isn’t helping, that the pain doesn’t go away, and the emptiness and loneliness remain, this video is the answer for you.

You pray, read, seek God, and do everything you’re told, but nothing changes, and a question arises in your heart: is God not listening?

Here’s a simple and profound answer: God hasn’t abandoned you or forgotten you. Even when you don’t see results, don’t feel changes, and don’t understand what’s happening, He continues to work deep within—at your roots, in your soul, where healing, freedom, and new life are born.

Faith in God isn’t always an instant answer to a request; it’s a path through which God provides exactly what you need for growth and healing.

Don’t give up, don’t close yourself off, and don’t think you’re alone. Keep praying, keep seeking, and trusting: God is near, He is within you, and one day you will see the fruit of this quiet work.
