God says: ask simply. Honestly. With faith. But with an open hand.

Not like this: “Lord, do as I have decided, otherwise I will not believe in Your love.”

But like this: “Lord, I ask You for this because I am hurt, afraid, and important. But I do not see everything. You see. Your will be done.”

Such prayer is not weaker. It is deeper.
Because in it, a person brings to God not only a desire but also trust.
And trust is greater than a demand.

This is not simply a conversation with God. It is the humility of the soul before His love.
Jesus Christ taught: “Thy will be done.” This is not weakness. This is the highest spirituality. The Bible says, “Ask, and it will be given to you.” But what will be given is what leads to life—not what fear demands.
Christianity is not about ritual. Orthodoxy is not about form. Faith is about an open hand.
