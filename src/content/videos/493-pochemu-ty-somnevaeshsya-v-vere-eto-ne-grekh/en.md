Do you doubt and think your faith has weakened? This video is a word of encouragement for those who pray but feel no answer and seek God in silence. Doubt doesn’t necessarily mean a lack of faith: honest questions can be the beginning of a journey toward trust. Weak faith is not a failure, but a beginning. Hear the prayer: “Lord, help my unbelief”—and turn to God as you are.
