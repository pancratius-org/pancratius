You think joy is when everything is good. But what if joy is when you’re not alone in the bad? God doesn’t ask you to rejoice in pain. He calls you to rejoice because He is with you in it.

Jesus endured the greatest pain—so that you would not be alone in yours. This is joy: not the absence of suffering, but the presence of the One who sustains you in it.
