You seek great things, but God asks for little. The Creator’s message: simply be with God. This is prayer, humility, and freedom. God’s words today are a spiritual awakening of faith. This is Christianity without theatrics.

At the end of the road, a person asks: “What should I do?”

The answer is simpler than you think.
Stand before God honestly. Say: “Lord, here I am.”
Give thanks for the gift. Repent of what you know.
Ask for light where you cannot see. Accept mercy.
Do the closest good. Renounce the closest lie.

This is repentance without theatrics. This is the Gospel without scenery.
God says: the healing of the soul lies not in the great, but in the small.

Don’t make a theater out of faith. Don’t make a means out of God.
Don’t make a source out of yourself. Don’t make a name out of sin.
Don’t make a master out of fear.

Be with God. This is not a little. This is everything.

Because if a person is with the Lord, they will go through much.
Through doubt. Through pain. Through darkness.
Through failure. Through loss. Through death.
If a person is without God, even abundance can become a desert.
