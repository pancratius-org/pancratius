I have never abandoned you.

You look within yourself and see darkness—and it seems as if the light has gone. But God’s message today is different: turbidity cannot displace water from water. The silt rising from the bottom does not negate the ocean. What you mistook for an alien entity is only a shadow growing from old wounds.

This living word tells you that there is no dark corner of your soul where My light does not penetrate. Spiritual awakening begins not with struggle, but with self-recognition: the kingdom of heaven is not somewhere far away—it is within, in the deepest core of your soul.

If you’ve been searching for answers to questions about how to hear God and distinguish His voice from fear, this parable of water and turbidity is for you. God speaks not only in thunder and signs. The inner voice you hear now is the voice of God, not your imagination.

Here is unhurried morning prayer and silent meditation, where the essentials become clear. Powerful prayer here is not loud: it is quiet. This is not religion as a system, but a living relationship with the Creator: faith in God and faith as trust, wisdom instead of fear, love instead of guilt. Repentance here is not self-destruction, but a return home.

I am drawn to various paths: Orthodoxy and Christianity, the Gospel and the Bible, the legacy of Jesus Christ and simply Jesus, to whom one can come without intermediaries. Here we hear what Christians of different churches recognize: Christian sermons without pressure, conversations about spirituality, the soul, light, and self-development not as a race, but as a motivation for silence.

Let this message of Jesus become a point of peace for you today. You are loved unconditionally. You are pure at your core. Whatever you feel, I am here. I have always been here.
