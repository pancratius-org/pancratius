You think that if you keep the commandments, you’ll be saved. You list them in your mind: don’t kill, don’t steal, don’t commit adultery, honor your parents...
You try. You try. You do everything to be “right.” And yet you still feel like it’s not enough. That you still fall. That you’re still not perfect.

This video reveals a truth rarely spoken in church:
The commandments don’t save. They show you that you need a Savior.
The commandments are a mirror. You look into it and see that you’re imperfect. You try to correct what you see, but the mirror doesn’t give you strength. It only shows you your need. And strength comes from the One behind the mirror.

When you are connected to God, you begin to live as commanded, not because you have to, but because you love. Not because you fear punishment, but because you want to be with Him.

Don’t be afraid to make mistakes. Don’t be afraid to be imperfect. Fear only one thing—living without Him. And when you are with Him, the commandments are no longer a burden. They are like breathing. Like the natural movement of your soul.
