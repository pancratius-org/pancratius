You think that Communion is just bread and wine in a church. But I tell you: the true Eucharist is your entire life, transformed into gratitude...
The meaning of life is not in a ritual, but in a living presence. You yourself become the liturgy when you allow grace to flow through you into the world. Communion is not a moment in a church. It is every look, every word, every touch, given with love.

If you seek faith in God as an encounter, and not as a rule, listen: repentance is not a look at your own filth. It is a look at My love. You repent not because you must, but because you are already loved. Baptism is not just water. It is a daily immersion in love, when you die to yourself and are resurrected for another.

This is spiritual awakening—when, through the question of how to find yourself, you come to unity, where God is near, where God’s love and God is love lead you along the path to God—through the Bible and the Gospel, in living Christianity and Orthodoxy. Where Jesus Christ is near, prayer becomes breath, and the soul finds peace and hope.
