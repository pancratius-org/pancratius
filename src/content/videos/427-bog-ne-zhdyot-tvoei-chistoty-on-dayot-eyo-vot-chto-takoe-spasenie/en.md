You think the prodigal son was saved when he reformed. When he became worthy. When he proved he had changed.

But I tell you: he was saved when he allowed himself to be embraced. Still in filth. Still in shame. Still without merit.

This video explains what salvation really is: salvation is not about reforming. Salvation of the soul is about allowing the Father to embrace you and accept the gift of being a son. Not for merit. Out of love. The Father doesn’t wait for your purity—He gives it.

These are not three events. They are one movement. A movement from darkness to light. From lies to truth. From loneliness to love.

You don’t have to become worthy to be saved. You must understand: you are already loved, even in your filth. And then your return to God begins—not as to a judge, but as to home.

If you ask how to be saved, the answer is not in effort. The answer is in an embrace. Let yourself be embraced. And you will remember who you are—a son. A daughter. Beloved. Already now, and not after death. This is the kingdom of heaven within you and life after death, which begins before death—with God’s love.
