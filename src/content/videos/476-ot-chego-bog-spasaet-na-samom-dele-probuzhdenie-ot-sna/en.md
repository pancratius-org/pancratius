We’re accustomed to thinking of salvation as deliverance from punishment, but God’s voice speaks differently. Salvation is a spiritual awakening, a quiet emergence from a sleep in which we’ve forgotten who we are.

If you’re familiar with loneliness among people, a strange longing for a home you’ve never seen, this is the memory of the soul. It’s a call that leads to a return home. You were never separated from Him, you never left His heart.

In this word about God’s love, you will hear how to free yourself from fear and inner emptiness, how to find peace and inner calm, how to hear the voice of the heart instead of the roar of anxiety. This message is for those who seek the meaning of life, who are tired of struggling for significance and righteousness, who want to feel that they are not alone.

God within you gently whispers your true name. Unconditional love already holds you. Faith in God here is not a transaction, but recognition. The Kingdom of Heaven is near, the Lord is near, and the soul can finally rest and see the light.

Allow yourself to believe: you are already saved. Not from fire, but from loneliness, from the illusion of separation, from the thought that you are unneeded and unloved. Home has been here all along. Just open your eyes—you are already home.

The prayer in this video is silence, presence, and trust. Listen as a Christian prayer of the heart, as a sermon about God’s plan for the wounded soul. May Jesus Christ, the Church of Living Hearts, and the New Testament of Love remind you of what matters most: you are a child, you are loved, you are home.
