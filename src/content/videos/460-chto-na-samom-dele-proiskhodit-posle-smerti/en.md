Death is the final step of birth. I await you in the home you’ve longed for all your life.

My child, you fear that death is the end, but I tell you—death is a return home. You already took this step once, when you emerged from the cramped, warm darkness into the light, into waiting arms. So now, the dark corridor leads not to nowhere, but to an embrace.

You ask what will happen after death and what awaits the soul after death. Hear God’s voice quietly within. Life after death is a meeting, not a judgment. The soul is not lost after death; the soul returns to the Father. There you will be met by all you loved, and by the One who is Love itself.

The fear of death is born from the thought that you are alone. But God is near. God is love, and God’s love never leaves you. My grace holds you, both in your last breath and behind the door. Peace comes to the heart when you understand that every tear and every loss was a journey home. The world seeks answers in words like clinical death, reincarnation, and past lives, but I reveal to you the meaning of life simply: you are a son, and you are awaited. Heaven is not a distant place; heaven is the Father’s house, where the soul’s salvation is already prepared, where eternal life begins with recognizing Me.

Let prayer become quiet trust, faith become peace, and hope become light. Living faith does not fear the door. Orthodoxy with the heart, Christianity with love, the Church as family—all living things lead to the Father. Open your heart to the Bible. Hear the Gospel not as a threat, but as the word of God spoken to you. Jesus speaks there. Jesus Christ breathes there.

Let spiritual development be a return, and let consolation be complete. I Myself will wipe away your tears. I Myself will meet you.
