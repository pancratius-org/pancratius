Are you afraid of death? You fear nothingness, separation, and the unknown—what comes after death, and that beyond the threshold there might be emptiness, darkness, or judgment.

This address is about the fear of death, familiar to every person, and about what happens to the soul when the body leaves this world. This video answers the question of what comes after death and whether there is life after death.

Death is not disappearance or the end, but a transition, like a door and like morning after night. You don’t disappear, you change form and return home.

What will happen to you after death? There you will not be alone. There awaits the One who has held your hand all your life. There is no emptiness or anger, but the Father who runs to meet you, and those you loved.

Faith, prayer, and the Kingdom of Heaven speak here not in the language of threats, but in the language of consolation: God will not abandon you in life, in death, or in eternity.

If anxiety about death is holding you back, if you fear loneliness and seek the meaning of life, this word is for you. Don’t be afraid, because you are not alone. I hold your hand at the threshold and will not let go.
