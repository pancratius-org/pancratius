You won’t become a son later. You’re already a son now.

My child, you’ve become accustomed to thinking of yourself as a slave and a debtor. That’s what the religion of fear taught, that’s what another sermon preached, where the church stood between the heart and heaven. But I speak differently.

Open the Bible with your heart. Look closely at the Gospel. Listen to the New Testament. Where Jesus speaks, where the voice of Jesus Christ resounds, where the Gospel of Luke breathes, one word resounds everywhere: there is. Not if. Not someday. But now.

The Kingdom of Heaven won’t come from outside, like a reward. It’s within you, like nature. It’s not a ritual or a matter of belonging to a particular side, be it Orthodoxy or another Christian denomination. It’s a living, spiritual awakening of sonship.

God is love, and He is already within. Prayer is not a distant cry, but the silence of recognition. Faith is not the fear of loss, but the peace of one who is already at home. Accept God’s Word not as a demand to become, but as a revelation to remember who you are by blood and spirit.
