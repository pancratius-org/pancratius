You’re carrying a grudge against someone who hurt you, didn’t apologize, and maybe didn’t even realize what they did. And a voice inside you says, “How can I forgive them if they didn’t ask for forgiveness and don’t deserve it?”

This video offers a Christian perspective on forgiveness and releasing resentment: why you, not them, need forgiveness; how forgiveness differs from justification; and how to let go of pain, giving it to God.

How to learn to forgive when there’s no apology; how to stop carrying someone else’s sin and open the door to the prison of waiting, where the soul stops breathing and living.

You deserve freedom, peace, and a life without this burden. Let go, not for them. For yourself. And walk free.

If this has touched you, write in the comments: I forgive and let go. And share this video with anyone who’s still waiting for an apology.
