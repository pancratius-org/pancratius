You think being born again is a metaphor. But I tell you: it is a process. It is liberation...
The meaning of life is not to become perfect. You don’t have to die to be born again. You must let go of what has been holding you back. You die to your fears. To your masks. To your expectations. To your ego, which demanded recognition. And you are born—to the truth. To freedom. To love. To silence. To Me.

If you are looking for a new start and how to find yourself—listen: this is not a one-time event. It is a way of life. You are born anew every time you choose to be alive. Not in the future. Now. You don’t stop being yourself—you stop being only this.

This is spiritual awakening—when the soul lets go of the past, faith in God becomes trust, and prayer becomes an encounter. Where God is near, where God’s love and God is love lead you on the path to God—through the Bible and the Gospel, in living Christianity and Orthodoxy. Where Jesus Christ says: do not be afraid, you are not dying—you are awakening. And peace of mind and hope are restored in the heart
