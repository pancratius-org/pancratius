You’re tired of guessing. Words are confusing. Thoughts are deceiving. But there’s a criterion that can’t be faked. It’s not in your head. It’s in your feelings.

When you’re driven by fear, your insides feel cramped. You shrink. Your shoulders rise. Your breathing becomes shallow. You brace yourself for impact.

When you’re driven by love, your insides feel spacious. You breathe deeper. You open up. Even when you’re scared, there’s room inside.

Fear speaks loudly, but it’s not the truth. Love speaks quietly, but it is the truth. You can learn to hear it with your body. Expand. It is He who leads you.
