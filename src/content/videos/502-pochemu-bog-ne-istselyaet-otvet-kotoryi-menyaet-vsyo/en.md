God hears every prayer and every tear. He doesn’t always take away an illness, but He never abandons a person to suffering. Instead of what is asked for, He often gives Himself—His presence, peace instead of pain, eternity instead of fear, and a life that doesn’t end with the death of the body.

If you’ve prayed for healing and haven’t received the answer you expected, this video will help you see weakness in a new light: as a place to meet God, a door to peace, and an invitation to stop fearing because you are not alone.
